Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

In short: AR Hoops does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There are no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics.

Camera

The app uses the camera through Apple's ARKit to place a virtual hoop and ball in your surroundings and to follow your shooting motion. Camera images are processed only on your device, in real time. They are never recorded, saved or uploaded.

Data stored on your device

Your best 3-Point Contest score is saved on your iPhone so it can be shown next time. It never leaves the device and is removed when you delete the app.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your 3-Point Contest scores are submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboard under your Game Center profile, where other players can see them. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; the developer only sees what Game Center shows on the public leaderboard. You can turn Game Center off in Settings › Game Center.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-up or login of our own.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No third-party SDKs, analytics or crash reporters. Apple may provide anonymous crash and usage statistics to the developer only if you have chosen to share them with app developers in iOS Settings.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a future version adds features that use data, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.

Support

Requirements

An iPhone that supports ARKit, running iOS 17 or later. Play in a well-lit space with room around you.

How to play

  • Move your phone slowly until the floor is found. A preview hoop appears in front of you and follows the direction you face; tap Set Hoop to lock it.
  • Your phone is your palm: the ball rests on top of it. Push the phone up and forward, and the ball leaves your hand as the motion slows down.
  • Practice: real-size hoop at the free-throw line; a passing machine sends the ball back so you can catch it.
  • 3-Point Contest: 5 racks around the arc (money balls worth 2), two deep balls worth 3, 70 seconds, 40 points maximum.

Troubleshooting

  • The hoop drifts or floats: scan the floor more slowly in better light, then tap the target icon to place it again.
  • Black screen: allow camera access in Settings › AR Hoops.

Contact

Report a problem or suggest a feature: GitHub Issues.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 23 日

简而言之：「AR 投篮」不收集、不在服务器保存、不分享、不出售任何个人数据；没有广告、没有跟踪、没有第三方统计。

相机

本应用通过 Apple 的 ARKit 使用相机，把虚拟篮筐和篮球放进你所在的空间，并识别你的投篮动作。相机画面只在你的设备上实时处理，不会被录制、保存或上传。

保存在你设备上的数据

三分大赛的最佳成绩保存在你的 iPhone 上，用于下次显示。它不会离开设备，删除应用即被清除。

Game Center

如果你登录了 Game Center，三分大赛成绩会以你的 Game Center 身份提交到 Apple 的 Game Center 排行榜，其他玩家可以看到。该过程由 Apple 处理，适用 Apple 隐私政策；开发者只能看到排行榜上公开显示的内容。你可以在「设置 › Game Center」中关闭。

我们不做的事

  • 没有自己的账号、注册或登录。
  • 没有广告，不使用广告标识符。
  • 不跨应用或网站跟踪你。
  • 不使用第三方 SDK、统计或崩溃收集工具。只有当你在 iOS 设置中选择与开发者共享时，Apple 才会向开发者提供匿名的崩溃和使用统计。

儿童

本应用适合所有年龄，不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的信息。

政策变更

若本政策变更（例如未来版本加入使用数据的功能），更新后的政策会在该版本发布前发布在本页面，并注明新的生效日期。

帮助与支持

设备要求

支持 ARKit 的 iPhone，iOS 17 或以上。在光线充足、周围有空间的地方使用。

玩法

  • 缓慢移动手机直到识别到地面。预览篮筐出现在你前方，随你的朝向移动；点「固定」锁定位置。
  • 手机就是你的手掌，球托在手机上方。把手机向上向前推出，动作减速的瞬间球离手。
  • 「练习」：罚球线距离的真实尺寸篮筐，发球机会把球传回来，迎上去接住。
  • 「三分大赛」：三分线外 5 个球架（money ball 2 分）、2 个 3 分的深远区球，70 秒，满分 40。

常见问题

  • 篮筐漂移或悬空：在光线更好的地方更慢地扫描地面，然后点准星图标重新放置。
  • 黑屏：在「设置 › AR 投篮」中允许使用相机。
